# v0.1.7

Extraction shortcuts now toggle the floating picker. Invoking the action again from the picker or its source tab cancels the existing search, instead of reporting that the extractor is already open or starting a second picker. Cancellation follows the Escape path, restoring the source focus and any saved zoom.

Other tabs' pickers remain open. Preview and direct selection remain independent of the UI. A completed diagnostic picker can also be dismissed with the shortcut. Copy and insertion behavior, extraction scopes, and bounded concurrency are preserved.
